Eastern Congo Ebola epidemic to continue to outpace previous outbreaks amid containment challenges
Director General of the World Health Organization (WHO), Dr Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus, on 16 July stated that the Ebola epidemic in eastern Congo is progressing at an “alarming” rate.
