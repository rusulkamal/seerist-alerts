Eastern Congo Ebola epidemic to continue to outpace previous outbreaks amid containment challenges
Director General of the World Health Organization (WHO), Dr Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus, on 16 July stated that the Ebola epidemic in eastern Congo is progressing at an “alarming” rate. The outbreak has yet to reach its peak, with cases likely to continue to increase before the virus is contained, and the funding shortfall will remain the primary constraint to scaling up response efforts in the coming months.
As of 18 July, 2,344 cases and 930 deaths related to Ebola have been reported in Congo. The virus has spread into two additional provinces, though 95% of cases are still recorded in Ituri province. Only one other country, France, currently has an active case.
