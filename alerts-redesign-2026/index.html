Redesigning the alert email

This is a concept, not a build. Nothing here is scheduled or committed, and it is shared to get reactions while it can still change cheaply.

For eight or more named accounts the email alert is Seerist, so the inbox is the product. This is the redesign: how one alert reads, how it holds up across every event type, and what it looks like at a real day's volume. Everything after the exploration is live rendered output from a working template. Asset names in the previews are placeholders; the headlines, sources, locations and distances are real.

Subject lines

One rule, branching on how many items the email carries. Put the most specific real thing this email actually has into the subject, nothing generic, nothing invented.

One story
{Location} · {Headline}

e.g. Philadelphia, United States · Extreme heat in store for Philadelphia area on Wednesday
Location is the smallest available unit, city before state before country. No content type, ever.

A digest
{Alert name}: {count} new updates

e.g. Global Security & Political Risk Monitor: 45 new updates
The alert's own name, as the user named it, not a generic type label.
