Where each cap comes from. Location is capped at six values or about a hundred characters, whichever hits first, and reports the rest in locationsHiddenCount. Sources show three and report sourcesHiddenCount. Assets show six in two columns and report nearbyAssetsHiddenCount, which becomes a link rather than plain text because the rest are worth clicking through for.

Risk and Tags have no cap. They render in full however long they get. The Risk example above is a real value from your inbox, and it shows the problem: parents are listed alongside their own children, so six values carry three values worth of information. Worth capping, or worth fixing upstream.
